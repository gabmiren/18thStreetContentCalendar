The S&P 500 just closed in a correction. What does that mean?
A correction is a fall of more than 10% from a record high. On Friday, the S&P and the Dow both crossed it.
It took seven straight down days, and an 8.6% drop in one week.
The next line is 20%, a bear market. The Nasdaq-100, the index behind NQ, touched it during Friday's session.
A correction tells you how far prices have fallen. It can't tell you what comes next.
So size down, and know your exit before you enter.
