Watch this before you trade NQ.
Every point NQ moves is $20 on one contract.
So a 100 point move is $2,000, up or down.
On Friday, the Nasdaq-100 fell 1,488 points.
On one NQ contract, a move that size is almost $30,000.
The micro, MNQ, is a tenth of that. $2 a point, same chart.
Know the dollars before you take the trade.
